# B7 Enemy machines: shared brief

v2 combat inside the v1 run: enemies are machines (a core plus parts), you aim with a target order, broken parts cancel intents and drop salvage. Read `docs/rules.md` sections 1.5, 2 and 3 and 4.8 (the rules), `docs/content.md` section 3 (the data), and the project CLAUDE.md. The contract is commit `effba46`: `src/core/types.ts` (search "B7"), `src/core/defs.ts` (`FrameDef`, `EnemyPartDef`, `WardenPhaseDef`, `Passive`, the new `TickCtx` words), `src/core/frames.ts` and `src/core/salvage.ts` (stubs that throw), `src/core/content/balance.ts`, `src/sim/strat/v2.ts`, `src/core/testkit.ts` (inline frames, `put`, test parts). ## Semantics every lane relies on (decided; don't re-decide)
- **Enemy turns are counted per enemy** in `turnsActed` (1 on its first turn; reset to 1 at each warden phase after the phase action). Cadence 'odd' acts on turns 1, 3, 5...; `{ of: 3, at: [1, 2] }` on turns 1, 2, 4, 5...; 'once' on turn 1; 'passive' never.
- **Intents** for the next enemy turn are computed at combat start and at the end of each enemy turn: one `PartIntent` per unbroken, unjammed part that will act (countdown parts show their countdown as the label, e.g. "Pierce 25 in 2"), in frame order. If no part will act because every acting part is broken, one `core` intent with the core action. A turn where parts simply rest is quiet (no intents). `e.intent` is kept equal to `intents[0]` (or a quiet `special` intent) and `c.targetIdx` to the enemy of `currentTarget(c)`, for v1 consumers.
- **Breaking** a part during the player's turn cancels its intent at once (remove it from `intents`, emit `partBroken`); if that leaves no acting parts, the core intent appears for the coming enemy turn.
- **Damage to a target, in order**: Strike amount (with boost, Grit, Knuckles as v1) -> Cracked x1.5 (floor) -> Governor cap (not Drill) -> Bulwark halves damage to the core (floor; not Drill) -> Shell absorbs (not Drill) -> Braced cap (wardens' keystones: half max HP per player turn, rounded up; last-phase core: a third, rounded up; tracked in `tookThisTurn`/`coreTookThisTurn`) -> HP. Anything past the target's remaining HP or a cap is lost: emit `lost` (or `braced` for the Braced part) with the amount and `part`. Damage to a sealed core never happens.
- **Strike target**: `currentTarget(c)` = first standing entry of `c.order`, else the front of the leftmost living enemy. **Sweep**: the front of every living enemy. **Shatter/Jam/Pry** use the enemy of `currentTarget`. Default order at combat start = `defaultOrder(c)`; the order is not refilled automatically after that.
- **End of the player's turn** (after the machine, before enemies act): Ratchet adds Strength x for every standing Ratchet part; then warden phase changes (rules 4.8: if a phase's last keystone broke this Run, the next phase's parts unfold, `phaseActionPending` = true, the core unseals if `coreExposed`); Scald etc. as v1.
- **Enemy turn**: a warden with `phaseActionPending` performs only its phase action, then cadences restart. Otherwise, in frame order, each part whose cadence says so acts (jammed: skip once, clear the jam; a jammed countdown doesn't tick). Countdown parts tick every enemy turn and act at 0 then reset. Build-up parts add `buildUp` + Pressure this enemy drained this turn, act at `>= to`, then 0. `escalate` adds to the first action's amount each time it acts (back to base at `escalateResetAt`). Corrode removes ceil(pct% x Plating) before attacks; Pierce ignores Plating; Siphon heals the core by the Plating it removed (up to its amount); rebuild restores the named (or first) broken part at ceil(maxHp/2) and removes it from `c.broken`.
- **Salvage**: when a part with a `salvage` breaks, push a `SalvageItem` to `c.broken` (locked is decided at settle time). When a core dies, its standing parts count in `c.wrecked`. Payout: scrapped 3, locked 6, wrecked 1 each (constants in salvage.ts). In B7 the run pays Scrap as Cogs; the run's post-fight Pending becomes `{ kind: 'salvage', ... }` (elites still give their trinket choice and blueprint as v1).
- **Legacy enemies**: defs without `frame` (the three wardens until B9, the tutorial enemy, summons not yet ported) keep v1 behavior, presented as a core-only enemy with one `core` intent.
