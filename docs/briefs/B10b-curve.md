# B10b Modes, Overwind and the curve: shared brief (draft for the critic)

The second half of the roadmap's B10 (D-043). It adds the four difficulty modes and the clock tower door, the ten Overwind twists, the hard achievements, and retunes the game to the curve. This is the owner's first playtest ask ("won in 4 runs; more difficulty"). Rules: `docs/rules.md` 5.7 (modes, Overwind), 5.8 and 7.4 (the curve, enforced by the simulator); data: `docs/content.md` 7 (h-* achievements), 9 (Overwind twists), 10 (prices); acceptance AD4, AD5, BV1, BV2, BV5, BV10 (`docs/acceptance.md`). Carried in: the expert route wins 9 to 18% with no meta against a target under 5% (B8 to B9b), the in-game autoplay won 3 of 6 real runs (B9b gate), a Plating route wins 0% (anti-turtle overshoots), the Clockmaker's memory is constant for burst bots, act 2 arrival HP fell with B10a's events (D-044), `coreTookThisTurn` should reset at turn start (B9a, D-041), the bots can't use Night Watchman, Free Pawl or Cascade Piston.

## Semantics (decided; don't re-decide)
- **Modes** (rules 5.7 table): Apprentice, Journeyman (default), Master, Clockwork, as data in a new `content/modes.ts` (enemy HP %, enemy damage %, hours per act, oil heal %, Brass %). Applied through `runConfigFor` and `createCombat` (multipliers rounded half up on every enemy part, core and action amount; hours per act; the oil station's heal; Brass at `finishRun`). Unlocks: Apprentice and Journeyman from the start, Master after a Journeyman win, Clockwork after a Master win (`profile.modesUnlocked`). The chosen mode persists as `profile.lastMode` and shows on the Spire gate and the run's HUD.
- **The clock tower door** (the Bellfoot place built in B10a): pick the mode and the Overwind level; locked entries say what opens them. Overwind is locked until the first win on Journeyman or harder (an Apprentice win doesn't open it, AD5); then levels open in stages: 1 to 3 at the first Journeyman-or-harder win, 4 to 5 with h-master, 6 to 7 with h-clockwork, 8 to 9 with h-ow5, 10 with h-ow8 (`profile.rewards.overwind` holds the highest open level). Level N applies twists 1 to N and +10% Brass per level.
- **The ten twists** (content.md 9), each a pure function of the run config, applied where its rule lives: traders +15% (rooms), one fewer hour per act (section), enemy parts +20% HP (combat start), the first placed part each combat Rusted until the next turn (combat), elites step twice on every third move (section), oil heals half (rooms), one salvaged part kept per combat and the rest scrapped for 2 (salvage), every enemy attack +2 (enemy), wardens gain a memory-style extra part chosen by the player's plan (frames), the Thirteenth Hour (the Clockmaker's fourth phase per content.md 9: Thirteenth Chime and Hourless Dial keystones, HP 30 each, Braced to 15; Plating lost at the start of each of his turns; the core reopens at 40 HP Braced to 13 with no Governor; its beat line).
- **Hard achievements** become available (content.md 7): h-master, h-clockwork, h-ow5, h-ow8, h-ow10, h-master-bare, with their checks in `checkAchievements`; their Legendary rewards and Overwind stages apply. After this phase all 33 are available.
- **The curve** (rules 5.8, measured on Journeyman, BV1, BV2, BV10, BV5): the expert route wins under 5% with no meta and the greedy under 2% (BV1, 300 runs); 100 expert careers on the sensible upgrade path have a median first win between run 8 and 12 (BV2); 100 greedy careers win by run 20 at the median (BV10); the offer-based impact of the most impactful part is at most 2x the median (BV5). Achievements are part of the career sim (they unlock items into the pool as a player would earn them).
- **Tuning levers, in this order** (each round logged in DECISIONS.md and a dated balance report): (1) the engine fix: reset `coreTookThisTurn` at the start of the player's turn, then the Clockmaker's Governor cap back to 14 if BV4 holds; (2) attrition: regular and elite damage per act, oil heal, starting HP; (3) the economy: Scrap from fights, trader prices, Brass per run (BV2's pace); (4) Plating viability: the Corrode percentages and the Plating bypass shares (content.md 3.0) until a Plating route wins at least a third as often as the burst expert (no plan is dead, D-029); (5) part numbers only for BV5 outliers. Warden numbers change only to keep BV4.
- **Bots first, tune second**: before tuning, the bots lane extends the drafter so the expert can value Night Watchman, Free Pawl and Cascade Piston, adds the greedy route (always takes the highest-rarity offer, never rests), and confirms the in-game autoplay and the simulator agree (the autoplay won 3 of 6 real runs against the simulator's 9 to 18%: find out why before trusting either).
- **The memory**: with a working Plating route the plan history varies; a test asserts that each of the four memory parts is chosen at least once across the bots' careers (Pressure and statuses through a forced-plan bot if no route plays them naturally).

## Round 2 (review/B10b/draft-verdict.md, FAIL 6.75): these decisions supersede the sections above where they differ
- **Split (M7)**: B10b is now **modes and Overwind only** (one lane, modes-overwind, plus review and gate). The curve becomes **B10c The curve**, after B10b merges, so the two never edit `enemies.ts`, `rooms.ts`, `meta.ts` or `combat.ts` at once. The curve sections above move to B10c's brief, amended below.
- **Application order and rounding (M5)**, amends content.md 9: enemy HP: the mode's HP % times Overwind 3's +20% on parts, multiplied, rounded half up once at the end, on every part and core. Enemy damage: the mode's damage % applies to amounts the player takes (Attack, Pierce, Siphon, Countdown and Build-up payloads), never to Shell, Mend, Bulwark, Governor or Drain; Overwind 8's +2 is added after the multiplier. Overwind 5 reads "on every third hour spent, elites step twice" (elites step per hour since B8). Overwind 7: the Scrapper's and the Tow Hook's extra offers are not limited by it. Brass: the mode's % times (1 + 0.1 times the Overwind level), multiplied once at `finishRun`.
- **Overwind 9 (M4)**: the Foreman and the Queen gain the extra part chosen by the player's plan as the Clockmaker's memory does (from `memoryParts`, shared); with no plan history they gain none. The Clockmaker is unchanged (he already has one). The markers use the fallback position until B11 adds a `memory` anchor to each warden rig.
- **The Thirteenth Hour (M3)**, a **fourth** phase (content.md 9): with Overwind 10, the Clockmaker's Midnight is not his last phase. When Midnight's core reaches 0 it does not die: the phase change runs as if its last keystone broke (the beat "There is one more hour. I kept it for you.", the phase action), the core re-seals behind Thirteenth Chime and Hourless Dial (HP 30 each, Braced to 15; Pierce 18 every turn; Rewind 3 combinations), and at the start of each of his turns the player's Plating is lost (Sprocket's Blanket keeps Plating only at the player's own turn start, so it doesn't help here). Midnight's core stays Braced to 26 as now. When both keystones break, the core reopens at 40 HP, Braced to 13, no Governor. The new parts' markers use the existing bell and head-dial anchors until B11. A real-engine unit test drives the fight through all four phases and asserts the floor (each phase at least 2 turns, the last at least 3).
- **Ladder (M6)**: a sim test runs the expert 300 runs per mode and asserts win rates strictly decrease Apprentice, Journeyman, Master, Clockwork; Overwind is verified by unit tests of each twist's application and the Thirteenth Hour test, and by the owner.
- **The clock tower door**: locked modes and levels say what opens them; the run saves its mode and level (shown on the HUD) and a reload keeps them.
- **B10c The curve, decided now so the hand-off is clear** (its own draft follows B10b's gate):
  - **Measure first (M1)**: B10c.0 publishes v2 baselines for BV1, BV2, BV10, BV5 and reconciles the in-game autoplay (3 of 6 real runs won) with the simulator (9 to 18%), freezes the drafter extension (Night Watchman, Free Pawl, Cascade Piston, a greedy route) before any lever moves, and moves the heavy sims behind a `test:curve` script.
  - **Levers** add the bench upgrades' costs and effects (BV2's pace). **Check order per round**: BV1 and the fight targets (BV3, BV4, BV8, BV9) after every change; BV2 and BV10 only at a round's end.
  - **Plating viability (M2)** replaces "a third as often": a Plating-heavy expert (the expert's combat with a Plating drafting bias, not the turtle) reaches act 2 at least 60% and act 3 at least 50% as often as the base expert, over at least 600 runs. The memory-variety check becomes a forced-plan unit test of `mainPlan` and `memoryPlan`, not a bot assertion.
  - **The `coreTookThisTurn` reset** is its own step with its own test and a BV4 rerun before any other lever.
  - **The warn floor (M8)**: B10c may close with a `warn` only if the expert's no-meta win rate is at most 8%, the career e2e's fresh-save autoplay first wins no earlier than run 5, and BV3, BV4, BV8, BV9 and BV11 are green; otherwise it fails and stays open. The report also gives how many Masterworks a median career has unlocked by its first win.

## Round 2 additions (review/B10b/draft-verdict-round-2.md, PASS 7.5)
- **Ladder test**: over 300 expert runs per mode, the win rate does not increase from Apprentice to Clockwork, the mean act reached strictly decreases, and Apprentice's mean act is at least 0.5 above Clockwork's. Re-validated after B10c's retune.
- **The Thirteenth Hour, completed**: of Midnight's parts, the Hour Wheel and the Midnight Bell stay into phase 4 if standing; the Governor Frame retracts (`lastPhase: 3` under Overwind 10, matching "no Governor"). The Plating loss happens only in phase 4, at the start of his turn, before his Rewind and his attacks. The fight's win check treats a Midnight core at 0 HP with the phase change pending as alive. The new parts pay Brass 4 and count for h-whole-clock.
- **Order on a hit the player takes**: the base amount times the mode's damage %, rounded half up; then Strength; then Overwind 8's +2; then the player's reductions (Dazed and the like); then Plating. Mode HP scaling covers summoned enemies too (a test).
- **Interactions**: Overwind 6 halves a heal after the mode's oil % (oil stations and Oil Flasks alike: round half up of base times mode % times 0.5). Hours per act = the mode's hours, minus 1 with Overwind 2, plus 1 for act 3 with the beacon, never below 6; a bounds test runs all four modes with and without these, and CL1 holds. The first Journeyman-or-harder win opens Overwind 1 to 3 inside `finishRun` (`rewards.overwind` = max(current, 3)); a test proves an Apprentice win doesn't. `RunRecord` carries `mode` and `overwind`. Overwind 4's Rust is an ordinary Rust (trinkets that clear or prevent Rust work on it as usual).
- **Defaults unchanged**: Journeyman at Overwind 0 gives exactly the numbers of today; a regression test compares a seeded Journeyman run before and after (the BV baselines for B10c stay valid).
- **Build order and fallback** for the single lane: modes and the door; twists 1 to 8; the achievements; twist 9; the Thirteenth Hour last. If the lane overruns, the gate may ship without the Thirteenth Hour (h-ow10 stays unavailable), logged and carried to B12.
- **Gate checklist**: `cheat.setMode` and `cheat.setOverwind` are documented in the gate notes so the owner can try the hard modes; Overwind 9's fallback marker is checked visible in the browser check.
