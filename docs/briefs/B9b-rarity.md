## Semantics (decided; don't re-decide)
- **Every item works.** No "Coming soon" text survives: each of the 21 items has its content.md effect and upgraded effect and a unit test that exercises the effect.
- **Carry-over exceptions** (amends rules 2.3 and data-model invariant 8 in the contract commit): only the Cascade Piston (once per its own Strike), the Overrun Coupler (once per turn, content.md 5) and the Apprentice's Hands (the second entry of the order takes half) send damage past a target, to the next standing entry of the player's target order. A carried hit never carries again; the Piston and the Coupler can each carry once in the same turn. Invariant 8 becomes "never carried past a target, except by these three items".
- **Foresight Dial** (content.md 11, question 3): v2 intents come from part cadences, which are deterministic, so the second turn is computed: `previewIntents(combat, enemyIndex, 2)` lists each standing part's action on the enemy's next-but-one turn (action and amount; random targets are not named). Recomputed whenever the state changes (a break, a phase change, a Rewind, a Jam), so never stale. Shown dimmed beside the current intent.
- **The Inventor's Watch** (content.md 11, question 4; wording of content.md 5 and rules 1.6 and 6 updated in the contract commit): once per combat, after a Run's replay ends, "Wind back" restores the snapshot taken just before that Run: board, charges, Pressure, HP, statuses, every enemy, the hand, the draw order, every random stream, and the fight's tallies (`planAcc`, the run-fact counters of that fight). So the same draws and intents follow and nothing from the rewound Run is counted. `watchUsed` lives outside the snapshot. **Defeat waits**: when a Run ends in a loss and the Watch is unused, the controller shows "Wind back" or "Accept defeat" before `settleCombat`; accepting settles as now. Not offered after a won fight. The snapshot lives in `CombatState`, so a reload keeps it.
- **One pool** (`src/core/pool.ts`): `eligible(profile, run, { kind, tier, family? })` returns unlocked ids only; `rollTier(run, stream, odds)`; `wardenCoreReward(run, profile, wardenId)`; `canTakeLegendary(run)`. Every source calls it: salvage, trader stock and trinket (content.md 1 odds), fuse candidates, vaults (act 3 Legendary when allowed), warden cores, the boss trinket choice (may include Masterwork trinkets), events (never Legendary). A test per source.
- **`RunState.legendary: string | null`** replaces data-model's `legendaryTaken`; invariant 9 becomes "at most one Legendary part or trinket per run". Updated in the contract commit.
- **The Queen's core**: `options` = up to two eligible Legendaries (parts and trinkets, unlocked, none held). Two: pick one of two. One: a single card to take. None: the normal reward, a Masterwork if any is unlocked, else a Rare (content.md 1). New `Pending` kind `{ kind: 'legendary', options: string[] }` with its screen; no skip.
- **Achievements**: all 33 from content.md 7 with `available`. **Available (22)**: e-first-win, e-pet, e-bell, m-act2-breaker, m-bell3, m-quick-foreman, m-break-all, m-all-chassis, m-calm-steam, m-status, m-burst, m-bells, m-vaults, m-salvager, m-fuse, m-drill, m-shatter, m-wrecker, m-three-elites, m-no-plating, h-flawless, h-whole-clock. **Open with Bellfoot in B10 (11)**: e-resident, e-lore, m-residents, m-lift, m-beacon, h-master, h-clockwork, h-ow5, h-ow8, h-ow10, h-master-bare; shown locked with "Opens with Bellfoot". At this gate the reachable Legendaries are Sprocket's Blanket and Sprocket's Whistle; Mirror Gear (m-residents) is the one Masterwork waiting on B10. `__game.cheat.unlock(id)` for tests and the owner.
- **Rewards with no system yet** (verdict M5): `profile.rewards = { journal: string[]; collars: string[]; landmarks: string[]; overwind: number; chassis: string[] }` records every reward now; the trophy shelf lists them ("Red collar: Sprocket wears it in Bellfoot"). B10 applies collars, landmarks, journal pages, Overwind and the Scrapper. Part and trinket rewards apply now (into the pool).
- **Achievement facts, each with its definition and owner (progression lane)**:
  - from the fight log in `recordFight` (event flags, no `machine.ts` edits by progression): parts broken by the player (retracted parts are not broken; a rebuilt part broken again counts again), the best single-turn damage, Scald damage this fight, a Drill breaking a part protected by Shell, Bulwark or Governor, three parts of one enemy broken by Shatter in one turn, warden turns, warden fully broken ("every part" = every part that stood at any point in the fight, the memory part included and the Apron too, so it must be broken before it retracts), HP lost in a warden fight (damage absorbed by Plating doesn't count), overpressure happened, cores killed with 2+ parts standing. If the events lack a flag needed here (word used, protection), the contract adds it to event emission (orchestrator, in `machine.ts`).
  - at the run's end from state: Steam parts in the bin, total Plating gained (`stats.plan.plating`), the chassis of a win.
  - where they happen (progression adds one counting line each): bells and their hours left (`section.ts` `ringBell`), fuses, vaults opened per act (`rooms.ts`), elites defeated per act (run settle), parts broken in act 1 before act 2, Sprocket pets (`controller.ts` `petSprocket`).
  - across runs in `profile.achievementProgress`: pets, bells with 3+ hours, parts broken in total, wrecking wins, per-chassis win flags.
- **`finishRun` is a fixed sequence** (one owner per step): RunRecord and Brass (existing) -> planHistory (B9a) -> `checkAchievements(profile, record)` -> unlocks into the pool and `rewards` -> one save write (invariant 10). A feat met mid-run unlocks at the run's end.
- **Tier marks** (AD6): color plus a shape: Common none, Uncommon one notch, Rare a diamond, Masterwork a cog, Legendary a star. One `TierMark` component on every item card (hand, board tooltip, salvage, trader, fuse, vault, reward, trophy shelf, bin). Added to `docs/art-direction.md`.
