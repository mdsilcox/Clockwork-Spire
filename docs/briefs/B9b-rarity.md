# B9b Rarity and achievements: shared brief (draft for the critic)

Add the Masterwork and Legendary tiers with working effects, one pool that every item source goes through, the 33 achievements with their unlocks, and the trophy shelf. Rules: `docs/rules.md` 4.4, 4.5, 4.7, 5.6; data: `docs/content.md` sections 1, 2 (the 10 Masterwork and 5 Legendary part rows), 5 (the 4 Masterwork and 2 Legendary trinkets), 7, 10, 11; shapes: `docs/data-model.md` "Version 2" (`AchievementDef`, `achievements`, `achievementProgress`, invariants 9 and 10). Owns acceptance AD1 to AD3, AD6, AD7, plus one behavior test per new item. This brief answers the B9 draft verdict (review/B9/draft-verdict.md): B1 (item engine owners), B2 (one owner per function), M4 (rarity half), M6, M7, S3, S5.

## Semantics (decided; don't re-decide)
- **Every item works.** No "Coming soon" text survives this phase: each of the 21 items has its content.md effect and upgraded effect, and a unit test that exercises the effect, not just the catalog.
- **Exceptions to "no carry-over damage"** (B7): the Cascade Piston, the Overrun Coupler and the Apprentice's Hands route damage to the next standing entry of the player's target order exactly as content.md says, once per Strike. Nothing else carries.
- **Foresight Dial** (content.md 11, question 3): v2 intents come from part cadences, which are deterministic, so the second turn is computed, not rolled: `previewIntents(combat, enemyIndex, turnsAhead: 2)` lists each standing part's action on the enemy's next-but-one turn (action and amount; random targets such as "a part" are not named). It is recomputed whenever the state changes (a part breaks, a phase changes, a Rewind or a Jam), so it never shows a stale turn. Shown dimmed beside the current intent.
- **The Inventor's Watch** (content.md 11, question 4): once per combat, after a Run's replay ends, a "Wind back" button restores the snapshot taken just before that Run: board, charges, Pressure, HP, statuses, every enemy, and every random stream's state (rules 1.6). The hand and draw order are part of that snapshot, so the same draws and intents follow (no reroll exploit). Usable after a Run that ends in your defeat; not after a won fight. The snapshot lives in `CombatState`, so a reload keeps it (rules 6).
- **One pool** (`src/core/pool.ts`): `eligible(profile, run, { kind: 'part' | 'trinket', tier, family? })` returns unlocked ids only; `rollTier(run, stream, odds)`; `wardenCoreReward(run, profile, wardenId)`; `canTakeLegendary(run)` (no Legendary part or trinket held yet). Every source calls it: salvage (a locked salvage gives 6 Scrap and the journal note, SV2, as now), trader stock and trinket (content.md 1 odds), fuse candidates, vaults (act 3 Legendary when allowed), warden cores (content.md 1: Foreman a Rare, one in three a Masterwork if any is unlocked; Queen a pick of two Legendaries if allowed, else a Masterwork, else a Rare), the boss trinket choice (may include Masterwork trinkets), and events (never Legendary). A test per source proves locked items never appear and a second Legendary never does.
- **`RunState.legendary: string | null`** holds the run's Legendary (part or trinket id). The pool sets it when one is taken.
- **The Queen's pick of two**: a new `Pending` kind `{ kind: 'legendary', options: [id, id] }` and its screen: two cards, take one (it joins the bin or the trinkets); there is no skip, since the Queen's core always pays.
- **Achievements** (`src/core/content/achievements.ts`): all 33 from content.md 7 with id, name, condition text, tier, hidden, reward, and `available: boolean`. **Available now** (their feature exists): e-first-win, e-pet, e-bell, m-act2-breaker, m-bell3, m-quick-foreman, m-break-all, m-all-chassis, m-calm-steam, m-status, m-burst, m-bells, m-vaults, m-salvager (the reward is the Scrapper chassis: until B10 builds it, the unlock is recorded and the shelf says "The Scrapper arrives with Bellfoot"), m-fuse, m-drill, m-shatter, m-wrecker, m-three-elites, m-no-plating, h-flawless, h-whole-clock (22). **Not yet available** (B10 features: residents, landmarks, modes above Journeyman, Overwind, lore pages): e-resident, e-lore, m-residents, m-lift, m-beacon, h-master, h-clockwork, h-ow5, h-ow8, h-ow10, h-master-bare (11). An unavailable achievement shows on the shelf, locked, with its condition and "Opens with Bellfoot". So at this gate the reachable Legendaries are Sprocket's Blanket (h-flawless) and Sprocket's Whistle (h-whole-clock); the rest open in B10. `__game.cheat.unlock(id)` grants any achievement for tests and the owner.
- **AD1's "exactly one"** is a test: each Masterwork and Legendary id is the reward of exactly one achievement.
- **When achievements unlock** (AD2): conditions are checked from run facts by `checkAchievements(profile, record)` inside `finishRun`, so unlocks land in the same save write as the RunRecord (invariant 10). Counters that span runs (Sprocket pets, bells rung with 3+ hours, parts broken in total, wrecking wins) live in `profile.achievementProgress`. A feat met mid-run unlocks at the run's end, never mid-run (the reward reaches the pool next run).
- **Run facts** for achievements are collected by extending `recordFight` (B9a) and by counting where things happen (bells, fuses, vaults, elites per act): parts broken (broken by the player only; retracted or rebuilt parts don't count twice; a rebuilt part broken again counts again), the best single-turn damage, Scald damage per fight, a Drill breaking a protected part, three parts of one enemy broken by Shatter in one turn, warden turns, warden fully broken (memory part included), damage taken in a warden fight, cores killed with 2+ parts standing. One owner: the progression lane.
- **Tier marks** (AD6): color plus a shape, never color alone: Common none, Uncommon one notch, Rare a diamond, Masterwork a cog, Legendary a star. One component `TierMark` used on every item card (hand, board tooltip, salvage, trader, fuse, vault, reward, trophy shelf, bin). Added to `docs/art-direction.md`.
