Make the three wardens frame machines with visible phases, Rewind parts and the Clockmaker's memory; add the Masterwork and Legendary tiers (parts and trinkets) with their sources; add the 33 achievements, their unlocks and the trophy shelf. Rules: `docs/rules.md` 4.7 to 4.9, 5.4, 5.6; data: `docs/content.md` sections 1, 2 (Masterwork and Legendary rows), 3.2, 3.4, 3.6, 5, 7; shapes: `docs/data-model.md` "Version 2" (`phases`, `memoryParts`, `AchievementDef`, `achievements`, `achievementProgress`, `planHistory`, invariants 9 and 10). ## Semantics (decided; don't re-decide)
- **Phases**: the B7 Braced phase engine stays as is (WP1, WP8 green). Wardens move from the B8 legacy intents to frames: `EnemyDef.phases` per data-model. The phase action is the enemy turn right after a phase ends, shown as the intent first, with no attacks that turn. Cadences restart each phase; the Queen's Gauge reading carries over.
- **Rewind** (rules 4.9, v1 C6 to C9 kept): at the start of the Clockmaker's turn, the standing phase's Rewind part lifts the player's strongest combination (the placed parts that dealt the most damage last turn, a chain counted as one combination) back to the draw pile with charge 0, and he heals half that damage. Breaking the part ends Rewind for the phase. The phase 1 to 2 action Rewinds once even if the Tick Spring is broken. Tock Weight also sets Pressure to 0. Hour Wheel lifts two combinations.
- **Memory** (rules 5.4, BF4 core half): `profile.planHistory` holds the main plan of each finished run (`plating | burst | pressure | statuses`, by the largest share of `stats.plan`); the Clockmaker adds the memory part for the most common plan of the last three runs (ties: the latest run), none with no history. The archivist's note is one line on the act 3 start (Bellfoot's archivist comes in B10).
- **Warden cores**: Foreman: a Rare, or a Masterwork one time in three if any is unlocked. Queen: a pick of two Legendaries (part or trinket) if one is unlocked and the run holds none; else a Masterwork; else a Rare. Clockmaker: no part; his parts pay Brass 4 each.
- **Legendary limit** (AD3, invariant 9): at most one Legendary part or trinket per run in total. Sources: the Queen's core, or the act 3 vault when the run holds none. Never sold, fused or found in events.
- **Locked items never appear**: one shared pool function filters every source (salvage, traders, fuse, vaults, warden cores, events) by the profile's unlocks. A locked salvage gives 6 Scrap and a journal note (SV2, B7) as now.
- **Achievements**: all 33 are defined in data with id, name, text, tier, hidden and reward. Each Masterwork and Legendary is unlocked by exactly one. A condition met mid-run unlocks in the same save write as the RunRecord (AD2), via `finishRun`. Conditions whose feature doesn't exist yet (residents, landmarks, modes above Journeyman, Overwind, the Scrapper, lore pages) are defined now with `available: false` and evaluated from B10; the trophy shelf shows them like the rest, locked, with their condition text, so the full list is visible.
- **Run stats for achievements**: one function `recordFight(run, combat)` called from the run's settle path collects per-fight facts (parts broken, the best single-turn damage, Scald damage, Drill breaks through protection, Shatter triple, warden turns, warden fully broken, damage taken versus a warden, cores killed with 2+ parts standing). Bells, fuses, vaults and elites per act are counted where they happen. The contract adds the fields; each lane fills its own.
- **Tier marks** (AD6): each tier has a color and a shape mark (Common none, Uncommon one notch, Rare a diamond, Masterwork a cog, Legendary a star), on part tiles, cards, salvage, trader, fuse and the trophy shelf.
- **Art for the wardens**: the Foreman uses two stacked paintings (`cut.png` with the apron in phase 1, `cut-phase2.png` after), crossfaded by the `phase` mood. The Queen's broken gauge stays broken across her phase mood (WP7). Clockmaker death should-fix from A2: lift the head dial, soft glow. All A2 rigs (acts 2 and 3) are converted into the game now, since the conversion is mechanical and the RigHub already runs (B11 keeps scenes, parts and the title).
