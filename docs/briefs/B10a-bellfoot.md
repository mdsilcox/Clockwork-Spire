# B10a Bellfoot and the Spire's memory: shared brief (draft for the critic)

The roadmap's B10 is split (D-043), as B9 was: **B10a Bellfoot** (this brief: the town, residents, landmarks, the archivist, the rewards kept since B9b, the Scrapper) and **B10b Modes, Overwind and the curve** (rules 5.7, 5.8: the four modes, the ten Overwind twists, the hard achievements, and the retune to BV1, BV2, BV5, BV10; drafted when B10a gates, using B10a's town for the clock tower door). Rules: `docs/rules.md` 5.1 to 5.5; data: `docs/content.md` 6 (resident and landmark events), 7 (the achievements they open), 8 (the Scrapper); shapes: `docs/data-model.md` "Version 2" (`ResidentDef`, `LandmarkDef`, `Profile.residents`, `landmarks`, `journal`, `bestiary`, `collars`, `collar`). Owns acceptance 14 (BF1 to BF3, BF5, BF6; BF4 is green since B9a) and turns the achievements e-resident, e-lore, m-residents, m-lift, m-beacon to `available`. Answers the owner's playtest asks: a world to move around in, a title and town with atmosphere, lore, less Slay the Spire between runs.

## Semantics (decided; don't re-decide)
- **Bellfoot replaces the Workshop screen** after every run and on Continue. It is one street, wider than the screen on the phone (it scrolls sideways inside its own frame, the page never does), with places in this order: the Spire gate (start a run), the Workshop (bench, chassis rack, inventor's notes: v1's panels moved here unchanged), Sprocket's corner, the trophy shelf (B9b's Trophies, moved here), the archivist, residents' stalls (one per resident living there), the clock tower door (B10a: shows Journeyman and "Overwind opens after a Journeyman win"; B10b fills it). The tinker walks to a tapped place (or Left and Right arrow keys, Enter to open), Sprocket follows; every place is also one tap from a town menu button. Walking is skippable: a second tap opens the place at once. Opening a place shows its panel over the street.
- **Painted**: the tinker and Sprocket rigs (both in the manifest; Sprocket has idle, happy, sleepy, walk). The street itself is a painted layered scene (an A3 asset pulled forward, art lane below); until it passes art review the street draws code-drawn fronts with the same place anchors (D-033 fallback).
- **Residents** (rules 5.4; content.md 6): a resident event's choice sets `run.flags.resident = id`; at the run's end (won, lost or abandoned) `finishRun` adds it to `profile.residents` (same save write, invariant 10). Each stall's effect applies to every later run through `runConfigFor`: Oil Merchant (start with 2 oil: an oil flask item usable at any room, heal 20% each, from the existing oil rules), Apprentice (start with one random starting part upgraded), Lamplighter (each act's layout fully revealed), Hour Ghost (the archivist gains lore pages and full bestiary entries), Trader's cousin (one extra trader per act, placed by generation). A resident's event no longer offers the move once that resident lives in Bellfoot.
- **Landmarks** (rules 5.4): the repaired lift (`lamplighter` event, fix the lift): the Gearworks section gains a lift passage from the entry to a room on the top floor; an opened vault (`vault-wheel` event opened, or any vault opened when the m-vaults reward arrives): in later runs that act's vault is a known room and its guardian is a regular fight; the lit beacon (`beacon` event): act 3 starts at hour 0 with its layout revealed. Landmarks are `profile.landmarks`; generation reads them through `runConfigFor` (a `MapGenPatch`). The archivist's map shows each.
- **The archivist**: four tabs: Journal (pages from achievements and lore events, in order found), Bestiary (every enemy met: name, painting or silhouette if not met, its parts and what they punish; full text only with the Hour Ghost), the Clockmaker's note (B9a's memory line, moved here from the Workshop panel), the Map (the three acts' landmarks). Text from `content/story.ts` and `docs/lore.md`; American English, warm, curious, a little melancholy, short.
- **Rewards kept since B9b apply now**: collars (Sprocket's corner offers the earned collars; the chosen one is `profile.collar`, drawn on his rig as a code-drawn band on the neck anchor), journal pages (into the Journal), landmarks (above), the Scrapper (on the chassis rack once `m-salvager` is earned). Overwind stays B10b.
- **The Scrapper** (content.md 8): its 8 parts and passive (the first enemy part broken each combat is salvaged upgraded; once per combat you may keep one wrecked part's salvage). The passive lives in the salvage path.
- **Achievements opened here**: e-resident, e-lore (hear the Hour Ghost, read the Stopped Clock, the Empty Chair and the Unsent Letter: lore events in content.md 6), m-residents, m-lift, m-beacon become `available` with their checks; the shelf drops "Opens with Bellfoot" for them.
- **Save**: profile fields default for old profiles (`migrate.ts`); BF5 (Spare Cogs to Spare Scrap) is checked by a test.
