# Bellfoot scene: notes (B10a.3)

Style v3 (art/style.json). Pieces are generated with `gen.py` (ComfyUI SDXL, 4 seeds each), cut out with `cut.py` (rembg; framed pictures that fill their frame are kept as opaque rectangles with feathered sides), and assembled with `build.py` into three layers: `sky` (the Spire and the town at dusk, three skies blended, parallax 0.3), `street` (a dusk stone wall, cobbles with leaves and cogs, the seven fronts at their contract anchors on y 640, five empty stall frames 120 apart), `foreground` (lamp posts and a shade at the foot).
Picks: gate gate_seed2, workshop workshop_seed2 (cropped to the door and bench window), Sprocket sprocket_seed2 (a doorstep with a dog and a basket-like birdcage), trophies trophies_seed1, archivist archivist_seed2, stall stall_seed1 (the goods painted over), clock tower clocktower_seed3, lamps wall_seed1; skies sky_seed3 + sky2_seed1 + sky2_seed3.
Shipped: sky.webp 1800x800, street.webp 2400x800, foreground.webp 2400x800 (about 0.4 MB of the 0.6 MB budget). Not done: a 2x repaint (it would double the dimensions and bust the budget; the scene is shown at 1x to 1.6x), a fully painted back wall (a tinted stone texture stands in), per-place hover glow (code).

Stall awnings vary: teal, amber, plum, dark teal, sage.
