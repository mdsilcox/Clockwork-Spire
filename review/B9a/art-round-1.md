## Foreman: PASS
- Style 9: painterly teal and copper with soft ink, matches the trial bar. Read 9: heavy brute, clear at card size.
- Phase: the crossfade to cut-phase2 (belt torn open, plates cracked) is clean, no ghosting or seam at frames 10 to 14; steam crown plus rock chips read well. Death: slump into a hunched crouch, eyes go dark, head and shoulders drop, no smear or clip; reads as "powered down". Hurt and idle are fine.
- should-fix, attack frames 10 to 16: the extended arm is thin and reads as pointing or waving rather than a swing; the stomp at frame 18 to 22 carries the hit. Fix: widen the forearm weights or add a clenched hand/fist-forward windup, keep the stomp.
- should-fix, death frames 38 to 45: stops in a crouch, not a lie-down; acceptable, but a deeper sink (knees to the floor) would sell it.
- should-fix, left hand: stray thin dark cable scraps dangle beside the left hand in most moods (painted in the source). Fix: clean in cut.png.

## Boilermaker: REVISE
- must-fix, death (all frames 0 to 45): she stays upright; the only change is a slight forward tilt, a cooler dim tint and steam at the hat. Reads as a fade-out, not a defeat. No fold, no lost glow, no arms dropping, no burst visible at the gauge or crown by frame 6. Fix: a real collapse (head and hat dip 20+ px, torso bow 25 to 30 degrees, scepter and staff lowered or tipped out, a shrink in height of 6 to 8 percent), plus furnace glow fading to embers and a final puff.
- must-fix, attack frames 16 to 32: the "slam" is steam clouds along the floor and a tiny body squash; scepter and staff barely move, so there is no windup anticipation and the weapon never lands. Fix: raise the scepter arm in the windup (frames 8 to 14), bring it down on the impact frame, with the ground ring and clouds timed to that frame.
- should-fix, attack frames 26 to 32: steam clouds reach the card edge on the right and look clipped. Fix: shorter travel or fade earlier.
- should-fix, hurt: only a tiny flash and a very slight lean; nearly invisible at game size. Fix: larger recoil (about 12 degrees, 10 px).
- Phase (V steam jets from the gauge, crown plume) is the strongest mood: reads clearly. Style: the inked flat painting is consistent with the act 1 cast, but heavier line than the Foreman; fine as long as the Clockmaker and her match.

## Clockmaker: REVISE
- must-fix, death frames 30 to 44: the upper body slides sideways off the lower body; the chest block and shoulder mass drift left against the coat, a dark gap opens at the hip, coat and leg shards float, and the chest dial ends up beside the chest instead of on it. It reads as a mesh glitch, not a kneel. The A2 should-fix (lift the head dial, soft glow) is addressed: the head dial is clear and the warm glow is soft. Fix: shift the rotation pivot to the hip, cut the lean to 6 to 8 degrees, shrink the lean-driven x slide, hold the torso weights firm across the waist, and lower the body by shortening the leg region only.
- should-fix, attack frames 10 to 14: the left arm raises and the hand-arc is a thin ink-edged ribbon; the arm reads, but the chain pendulums and torn thin strands streak out sideways like hairlines. Fix: weaken the chain swing in attack or clamp it.
- should-fix, hurt: nothing moves except a star at the chest; frames 5 to 34 look like idle. Fix: lean, flinch or head snap in the first 0.4 s.
- known weak spot confirmed: legs do not bend, but only matters for death (see above). Not a defect elsewhere.
- Phase: the cold color shift, the two rings and the arm flare read well; no seams. Rewind: six strands with cogs reaching the palm is a clear read and clean, nothing clipped.
- Style: busy intricate inked painting, matches the "Spire machines are intricate" rule; thin stray pale wire edges around the chain loops remain at rest, mild.

## Ranked improvements
1. Boilermaker death: a real collapse and glow-out (currently the weakest mood in the batch).
2. Clockmaker death: re-pivot so the body bows over the hips without tearing.
3. Boilermaker attack: windup and weapon impact; then give both hurts a visible recoil.
