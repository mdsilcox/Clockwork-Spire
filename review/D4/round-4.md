# D4 art review, round 4 (owner notes on motion and sharpness)

## Verdicts
- Sprocket (happy): PASS (should-fix only)
- Cog Rat: PASS with should-fix (hurt 0 to 1 ghosting)
- Title: PASS (clock faces at 2x still slightly soft)

## Sprocket happy
- Mouth: a painted open mouth with a pink tongue and a dark mouth line, in the same ink style as the head. It reads as a real panting corgi in profile. Zoomed on frames 0 to 3, 44, 45, 87 to 89: no seam, no ghost double line, no color step at the muzzle edge. Frames 0 and 89 match (loop is clean).
- Body: calm. Tail wag, small rear sway, head up, ears back; no hop. Natural.
- Caveat: the recorded clip starts at full mix (frame 0 is already open-mouthed), so the crossfade itself (about 0.2 s fade from idle) is not in the frames. Not verified; check once in the live page (idle to happy switch) for ghosting.
- Should-fix: the soft ground shadow under the paws that idle has (idle/000) is missing in every happy frame. The dog floats a little when swapped from idle. Draw the shadow in the happy layer or the shared rig.
- Should-fix: frame 60 shows a closed squint eye with the open mouth; fine, but hold it 3 frames at least (it is one frame in the sampled strip). Minor.

## Cog Rat
- Attack 0 to 14 crouch, 15 to 19 dart (head leads, body follows, nose up to 40 px ahead of plate), 20 to 28 jaw snap and sparks, then recovery. No shear, no block sliding in the sampled frames; steps visible in legs at 17 to 19. Reads as a small animal lunging. Tail trails.
- Idle sampled every 6 frames: only small head, ear and tail movement, no sliding.
- Hurt: flinch at 2 to 8 (jolt right, head up, star on plate), skitter back 9 to 24, settles. No slides or shears.
- Should-fix (hurt 0 to 1): the tint is so strong that the legs, belly and tail become see-through, a ghost (round 3 had a solid pale rat). Lower the tint to about 0.25 and keep the legs opaque. Also frames 1 to 2 pop from ghost to full color in one frame.
- Should-fix (attack 15 to 19): the 120 px dart is only 5 frames and the legs mostly cross the ground in a stretched pose at 17; add one more gather frame so the feet visibly push off. Minor.
- Should-fix (hurt 5 to 11): a small brass cog bobs above the plate on a spring and then drifts away at 24 to 36 (a cog on the tail at 33 to 39). Intended effect, but it reads as a stray dot at 100 px.

## Title
- shot-desktop (1280x800) and shot-phone (667x375): sharp linework, clouds and skyline crisp; far clearer than the previous blur. Title text and buttons crisp.
- shot-clocks-2x (520x300): clock faces and the tower ironwork are still slightly soft; the numerals are legible but not crisp. The 2432x1664 repaint is the limit. Should-fix only: a sharpen pass on the clock faces or a 3x repaint of the tower.
