## Round 2 must-fix status
- Boss hurt 0 to 30: RESOLVED. Frame 0 to 2 is a light tint (still paler than idle but the figure is solid, not a ghost), an ink-edged star appears on the plate at 3 to 8, the body leans back and slides right by about 25 px, the head snaps, arms swing out, and it is settled by about 20. - Rat hurt 0 to 30: RESOLVED. Frame 0 is a solid pale-tinted rat with a bold ink-edged star on the shield (full size check: clean, no halo), then a big jolt right, hop and head snap (3 to 8), settling by 20. Clear even at 100 px.

## Should-fix status
- Steam, smoke, shrapnel flat ink-edged: LANDED. Puffs are two-tone flat lumps with ink edges; they now match D-030 and the cel look of Sprocket. Sparks on the rat are small ink diamonds. The Queen's additive spark lines remain (known), not visible as a problem in sampled frames.
- Phase haze: GONE. Phase 15 is clean, cracked dial intact.
- Queen slam weight: IMPROVED. Squash and hold visible at 38 to 48 with a ground ring and big puffs at the feet; reads at phone size (220 px strip, frame 42).
- Rat windup crouch: LANDED. Frames 8 to 16 sink the body onto the legs, tail drops, then a clear pounce at 20 to 30.
- Rat death belly shadow: LANDED. Frames 60 to 89 show a dark contact shadow, slumped body, cogs resting; steam and cogs start late as intended.
- Leg ink lines: not pasted on. At full size they read as part of the legs; at 100 px the legs are now visible instead of vanishing.

## New or remaining defects
- Boilermaker attack 60 (right edge) and 83: a gray-shaded puff is clipped by the frame edge at 60, and puffs at the head in 83 are a bit large for the head. Should-fix: keep puffs inside the canvas or fade before the edge.
- Boss steam sheets in phase 30 to 50 are big gray bands that cross shoulders and the clock; still clear of the face. Minor, should-fix: shrink them.
- Hurt frames 0 to 2 (both) remain a little pale at the tint; acceptable. Rat leg lines are slightly uniform but fine.
- Density gap versus Sprocket persists (enemies are denser, thicker outline); this is the open owner question, not a defect.
