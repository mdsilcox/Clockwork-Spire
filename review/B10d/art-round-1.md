# B10d.1 painted title: art review, round 1

Verdict: **PASS** (no must-fix). Scores: Style match 8, Read 8, Polish 7, Motion 7, Fit 7 (average 7.4; all at least 7). Phone composition is the weak point and should be fixed before the owner sees it.

## Judged
- Crispness: desktop (1280x800) is clean. Clock faces, lattice and ironwork hold at 2x; no visible blur, no banding. The D4 softness note is resolved. Phone (667x375) downsamples well; the tower still reads as detailed painting.
- Tower: fully visible at both sizes. Desktop: buttons sit in two columns either side, nothing touches the tower. Phone: the right column starts at x 428, which is right at the tower's edge; the color-blind checkbox clips the base of the tower slightly. Acceptable, but tight.
- Ambience (desktop): window and clock glow sit on the real windows and are subtle. Between 0 s and 3 s only small changes: good, nothing distracting. Steam puffs rise at the chimney tops near x 495/405/240 and drift; they look like flat round white blobs rather than steam wisps, and the one over the right skyline (x 1080, y 630) sits on a spire, not an obvious chimney. Lamp flicker is not noticeable in the stills. On the phone the ambience is almost invisible (fine).
- Composition (desktop): good, tower centered, title top left. Clutter: the notice bar, the color-blind box and the "Keys in a fight" box are dark slabs that fight the painting; "Practice fight" and "Practice sandbox" look like duplicates.
- Phone: eight full-size buttons plus the checkbox cover roughly 55 to 60 percent of the frame; the painting survives only as a strip in the middle and the bottom skyline. The wordmark is small.
- Delayed state (phone-delayed.png): the dark teal gradient with the real title, notice and buttons is clean and readable, and the layout does not jump (same positions). OK. No loading hint, which is fine.

## Fixes
1. (should-fix, phone) Reduce to one primary "Climb the Spire" (full size, left) plus a compact row or icon row for Save slots, Practice, Tutorial, How to play, Glossary, Settings (small pills, around 40 px tall, text 12 px+), placed at the bottom or in the right column narrower than 110 px. That frees the sky and the tower's right side.
2. (should-fix, ambience) Make steam softer and more irregular: lower opacity, vertical elongated plume with drift and fade, smaller start radius; move the x 1080 puff to a real chimney or drop it.
3. (should-fix, desktop) Remove the "Keys in a fight" box and the color-blind checkbox from the title (they belong in Settings and How to play), shrink the notice to one line; merge or rename "Practice fight" vs "Practice sandbox".
