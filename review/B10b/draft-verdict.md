## Can the tuning plan meet the curve?
Targets against what is measured: no-meta expert win rate 9 to 18% (target under 5%, BV1); in-game autoplay careers win on run 3 (a fresh save wins run 3, `balance/2026-10-05-v2-climb.md`) against a median first win of 8 to 12 (BV2); greedy median at most 20 (BV10, never measured on v2); Plating route 0% (new target). There is no v2 baseline for BV2, BV10 or BV5 in `balance/` (only v1 careers), so the size of the gap is unknown. The expert's win rate must fall about 3x while the career pace stretches about 3x, which is a coupled problem: BV1 is set by combat and attrition, BV2 by combat, attrition and how fast Brass and bench upgrades add power. Three rounds are plausible only if rounds are cheap and ordered by cost; they are not planned that way.

## Must-fix
M1. **Measure first, and put the meta in the levers.** Step zero of the curve lane must publish a v2 baseline: BV1 (300 runs), BV2 (100 careers), BV10 (100 greedy careers), BV5 (impact table), each with seeds, plus the autoplay-versus-simulator reconciliation the brief already promises (autoplay won 3 of 6 real runs and the career test wins on run 3 while the sim says 9 to 18%). The lever list changes enemies, oil, starting HP, Scrap, trader prices and Brass per run, but never the bench upgrade costs and effects (Reinforced Frame, Oiled Bearings, Spare Scrap, Second Wind and so on), which decide how quickly a career climbs from under 5% to a win. Add them, plus an explicit order of cheap checks: BV1 (about 2 minutes) after every change, BV3, BV4, BV8 and BV9 (these are fast and will break first when damage rises), then BV2 and BV10 (about 17 minutes each) once per round, BV5 last.

M2. **"A Plating route wins at least a third as often as the burst expert" is not a sound target.** (a) At the end of tuning the expert wins under 5%, so a third of that is about 1.5% or less: with 300 runs the standard error is about 1 point, so the test is noise; (b) the "plater" policy in `v2routes.ts` plays the turtle combat bot, a strawman that wins 0 to 0.3% by design (target 3 says the turtle must lose at least 1.5x the expert's HP; BV8 caps its full Plating absorption at 40% of enemy turns), so lever 4 pushes directly against BV3 and BV8 which are green and tuned; (c) "no plan is dead" (D-029) is a viability claim, not a win-rate ratio. Replace it with a measurable viability test: the expert drafting a Plating-heavy bin (not the turtle bot) reaches act 2 and act 3 within a stated factor of the base expert and its HP lost per act stays within 1.5x of it, over at least 600 runs, and leave BV3 and BV8 untouched. If the owner wants a win-rate ratio, state the run count that makes it measurable.

M3. **The Thirteenth Hour has no defined trigger.** Content 9 says that after Midnight the core "re-seals behind two keystones" and "reopens with 40 HP, Braced to 13". But in the engine a warden's last phase ends when its core dies (the fight ends), and phase changes happen when the last keystone breaks. So: when does phase 4 start (when the 78 HP core hits 0, at which point it is refilled to 40? or when the Hour Wheel and Bell break)? Is Midnight's core Braced (the rule makes only the last phase's core Braced, and with a fourth phase Midnight is no longer last)? The brief also says "a fifth-phase entry" while content says fourth phase. Decide, amend rules 4.8 and content 3.6 and 9, and test it: the new parts' anchors (Thirteenth Chime, Hourless Dial) need places on the Clockmaker's painting or a stated code-drawn fallback (AR2 requires an anchor for every part in a def), the phase beat line and phase mood, "Plating lost at the start of each of his turns" with Sprocket's Blanket (does the Blanket's kept half survive?), the floor of at least 2 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 3 = 12 turns, Hourless Dial's Rewind of 3 combinations (the `liftCombos` count), and a unit test on the real engine with a forced Overwind 10 fight.

M4. **Twist 9 is ambiguous and may not work on two wardens.** "Wardens start with one extra part, chosen from your plan as the Clockmaker's memory does": the memory parts have anchors only on the Clockmaker's painting. For the Foreman and Queen, state the anchor (a named generic anchor, or a code-drawn chip), that the Clockmaker gets a second memory-style part or none (he already has one from memory), what happens with no `planHistory`, that the extra part is not a keystone and not Braced, and that its salvage and Brass are as the Clockmaker's memory part.

M5. **Twist and mode application order and rounding.** "Multipliers rounded half up on every enemy part, core and action amount" would scale Shell, Mend, Summon counts, Corrode percentages and Drain; enemy damage should apply to attack-type amounts only, per hit, and each hit rounded separately (3 x 10 at 75% is 3 x 8, not 22). State the order of mode HP%, Overwind 3 (+20%) and Overwind 8 (+2 per attack: before or after the damage multiplier), that Braced caps follow max HP, and that Overwound's Strength 3 and Shell 10 are not scaled. Twist 5 says "every 3rd move" while B8's rule is that elites step on every hour spent; define the counter (moves or hours). Twist 7 (keep one salvaged part per combat, scrap the rest for 2) collides with the Scrapper's passive and Tow Hook; say which wins. Brass at the end: +10% per level combines with the mode's Brass % how (multiply or add)?

M6. **No test of the mode ladder.** Master unlocks after a Journeyman win and Clockwork after a Master win, and the hard achievements need wins at Master, Clockwork and Overwind 5, 8 and 10. Nothing checks these are reachable, and the career sim has no mode policy: BV2 and BV10 are defined on Journeyman, so the bot must stay on Journeyman for those and a separate measure should show a bot with meta winning Master. Add: the career bot's mode policy (Journeyman only for BV2 and BV10), a ladder run (cheat-profile, expert, 100 runs per mode) reporting win rates for Apprentice, Master and Clockwork with a stated expected ordering, and a note that Overwind 10 is verified only by cheat tests and the owner's playtest.

M7. **Lane coupling.** modes-overwind and curve both edit `enemies.ts` (a new phase entry against number retunes), `combat.ts` (marked blocks, accepted), `rooms.ts` (twists 1 and 6 against price constants), `meta.ts`. Run curve measurement and levers only after modes-overwind merges its application points (the contract's pass-throughs make the order safe), or give each file one owner per step; at least say that levers touch only numbers and never the lines the twists add. The `coreTookThisTurn` reset changes Braced semantics (B7 and B9a tests WP8, WP1): it needs its own unit test and a BV4 rerun before any other lever.

M8. **A `warn` at the gate must still mean the game got harder.** "Never a silent miss" is good, but if three rounds fail the owner's first playtest ask ("won in 4 runs") is unmet. Set a minimum bar for passing with a `warn`: for example expert no-meta win at most 8%, the autoplay career's first win no earlier than run 5, and BV3, BV4, BV8, BV9, BV11 still green. Below that bar the phase is not closed.

## Should-fix
- Test runtime: BV2 is about 3000 climbs and BV10 about 2000, tens of minutes each, and `npm test` runs `tests/sim/**`. Put the BV1, BV2, BV10 and BV5 sims behind a `test:curve` script (as `test:career` is separate), with `npm test` reading the latest committed report plus a fast sanity run; otherwise every phase's suite becomes unusable.
- The memory variety test ("each of the four memory parts chosen at least once across the bots' careers; Pressure and statuses through a forced-plan bot") tests the bot, not the game; also verify the plan-stat definitions still allow Pressure and statuses to be a main plan for a human (B9a's statuses and pressure buckets never won in sim). A forced-plan unit test on the stats is enough.
- The drafter extension (Night Watchman, Free Pawl, Cascade Piston) will move BV1 and BV5 together; do it, measure, then freeze the bots before any lever (the order is right, say it is a hard gate).
- Overwind levels and Brass: show on the clock tower door how many levels are open and why a locked level is locked (AD5 E), and that the chosen mode and level are saved in the run so a reload keeps them.
- h-flawless, h-whole-clock and the other 22 earned achievements change the pool in careers; report how many Masterworks a median career has unlocked by its first win.
