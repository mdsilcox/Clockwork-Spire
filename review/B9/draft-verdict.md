## Blockers

**B1. The Masterwork and Legendary effects have no engine owner, and two are undecided.** rarity-trophies owns `content/parts.ts` and `content/trinkets.ts` (10 + 5 parts, 4 + 2 trinkets). Content sections 2 and 5 make each of the 21 bend a rule: Skewframe (diagonal motion), Twin Mainspring (second motion source), Night Watchman (acts on the enemy turn), Cascade Piston and Overrun Coupler (overkill carries, which rules 2.3 and invariant 8 forbid by default), Perpetual Engine, Bottled Dusk (+2 ticks), Sun-Orb Core, Apprentice's Hands (two target entries), Two Left Hands (swap hand with board), Tow Hook, Foresight Dial, The Inventor's Watch (wind back a Run). These need hooks in `machine.ts`, `combat.ts` and `board.ts`; the brief gives `combat.ts` to wardens-core and `machine.ts` and `board.ts` to nobody. Content 11 "still open" items 3 (Foresight Dial shows two turns ahead, conflicting with rules 1.6) and 4 (Inventor's Watch: which RNG streams rewind, what the preview shows) are unresolved, and "decided; don't re-decide" is silent on both. AD1 to AD3 and AD7 test only the catalog, unlocks and the limit, so a green lane could ship all 21 as "Coming soon" stubs, as B7 did for four. Fix: decide which effects ship in B9 (all 21, or a stated subset), write a rule for each open question into rules and DECISIONS.md, give the engine hooks an owner (a `rule-benders` lane, or named hook points in the contract), and add one unit test per item (fires as its text says, invariants hold).

**B2. Shared functions are split across lanes.** `recordFight` is "the warden facts" (wardens-core) and "the rest" (rarity-trophies): one function edited by two lanes. `finishRun` must (invariant 10, AD2) write achievements, counters and `planHistory` in the same write as the RunRecord, yet wardens-core owns `planHistory` and rarity-trophies owns the `finishRun` unlocks. This is the same-interface coupling the lane rule says to turn into contract. Fix: the contract commit writes `recordFight` as a fixed list of calls into per-lane functions (`recordWardenFacts` in `src/core/frames.ts`, `recordPartFacts` in a new `src/core/stats.ts`) and `finishRun` as a fixed sequence calling `pushPlanHistory` and `evaluateAchievements` stubs, one owner each. Name `AchievementCtx` and every counter (bells per act, fuses, vaults, elites per act, parts broken) with its owner; "each lane fills its own" does not say which is whose.

**B3. The Clockmaker's memory has no defined input.** Rules 5.4 and the data model say `stats.plan` accumulates damage and Plating by source (Plating, burst Strikes, Pressure, statuses). `RunStats` has no `plan` field, the brief's RunStats list omits it, and no lane owns the accumulation. How a Strike, Sweep, Scald, Shatter, Echo or Plate is classed, what an early loss with near-empty stats records, and the within-run tie-break are unspecified, yet BF4 and WP6 rest on them. Fix: add an event-kind to bucket table to the semantics, name the owner, and test an empty run, a within-run tie, and a v1-migrated profile with no `planHistory`.

## Must-fix

M1. **Rewind and phase actions are incomplete against the docs.** The brief omits the Midnight Bell (Attack on odd turns; on even turns Jam the Mainspring and Attack 36; WP5, content 3.6) and the phase 2 to 3 action (Jam the Mainspring for the next turn). It names only the Foreman's and Clockmaker's phase 1 to 2 actions; the Queen's (Summon a Steam Wraith; then Mend, rebuilding the Gauge at half HP if broken and resetting its reading) are absent. Add them with part ids. (content 3.4, 3.6; WP2, WP5)

M2. **The phase data shape cannot hold the content.** `EnemyDef.phases[].beat` is one string, but the Queen's 2 to 3 beat depends on whether the Gauge is broken. The Gauge belongs to phases 1 and 2 while each phase lists its own `parts`; "gauges don't restart" (content 11 open item 6) needs an encoding (a `persists` list). Is the Gauge present in phase 3? The table says no, yet Mend rebuilds it at 2 to 3. Decide in the brief, extend the type in the contract. Also state the memory part is present from phase 1, non-keystone, so not Braced.

M3. **Contradiction: when the archivist's note shows.** The brief puts one line at the act 3 start. Rules 5.4, content 3.6 and WP6 say the note names the part before the run, and WP6 has an E clause. The archivist is B10. Pick one: a line in the v1 Workshop before the run (matches the docs), or the act 3 line with WP6's E clause deferred to B10 and logged in DECISIONS.md and acceptance. As written the "owns WP6 [U, E]" claim cannot be met.

M4. **Warden reward and HUD UI are unowned.** "A pick of two Legendaries (part or trinket)", the boss trinket choice with a Masterwork trinket replacing one slot for the Foreman and Queen (content 5), the phase-line display (WP2), and the Trophies entry point (Title or Workshop, `controller.ts` actions, `window.__game` hooks) have no owner and the `Pending` union has no warden-core kind. Assign `controller.ts`, `Combat.tsx`, the salvage UI and the Workshop entry; say where the trophy shelf opens from until Bellfoot (B10).

M5. **Art lane scope and a false premise.** "All A2 rigs converted now" is 15 or more assets plus the Foreman's second painting, `stage.ts` phase wiring and the Clockmaker polish in one lane. The brief says AR2 is "pulled forward from B11, see below", but roadmap-v2 gives AR2 to A1 and A2 (B11 owns AR1, AR3 and others) and nothing below explains it. Correct the sentence, update docs/roadmap-v2.md and DECISIONS.md in the launch commit (D-037 precedent), state that conversion is non-blocking for the gate (code-drawn fallback stays, D-033), that the AR1 size budget (250 KB per warden, 6 MB in all) covers the second Foreman painting, and that each asset gets an art-reviewer pass. Warden rigs first, the rest pipelined. Confirm the Foreman rig has anchors for his five new part ids and the Queen's for the Gauge (WP7).

M6. **The locked-item filter touches B8 code with no list.** `eligible(...)` must replace the filters in salvage, trader, fuse, vault, warden core and events (`rooms.ts`, `salvage.ts`, `shop.ts`, `eventfx.ts`); events are in no lane. List each call site and its owner, test each source (AD3 only checks totals), say where `run.legendaryTaken` is set, and that a held Legendary does not block a vault Masterwork (content 11 item 7).

M7. **Achievement scope is not tied to what is earnable at B9.** Only some of the 33 can fire (modes, residents, Overwind, lore, pets, Scrapper and landmarks wait for B10). At the B9 gate only h-flawless and h-whole-clock can unlock a Legendary, so 3 of 5 Legendary parts and one trinket are unreachable and the Queen-core and act 3 vault Legendary paths are nearly untestable by play. List the `available` set, add a `cheat.unlock(id)` hook for e2e and the owner, add `available` to the contract type (it is not in the data model; `check` is a function there), make AD1's "exactly one" a test, and name m-act2-breaker (Rare) and m-bell3 (Masterwork trinket) as AD7's no-win unlocks.

M8. **Required process steps are missing.** No art-reviewer pass, no browser-checker at 667x375 and 1280x800, no gate with a fresh critic, no step list for the board (B8's draft had all). Add B9.0 contract, lanes, bots, art review, browser check, B9.5 tune, gate.

Consider B9a (wardens, memory, their rigs, BV4) and B9b (rarity, achievements, trophies). If kept together, state what may slip without failing the gate (the 15 non-warden rigs; some Masterworks).
