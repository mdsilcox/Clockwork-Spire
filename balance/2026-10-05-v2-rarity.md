# v2 rarity: the pool with every available achievement earned (seeds 1 to 3)

Date: 2026-10-05. B9b.5, measurement only (no tuning). Runs are no-meta Journeyman climbs, Tinker chassis, 300 per policy per seed; the "rarity" profile has all 22 available achievements earned (`allEarnedProfile` in `src/sim/strat/v2routes.ts`), so every reachable Masterwork and both Sprocket items are in the pool. Items are locked by default: BV3, BV4, BV6, BV8, BV11 and the career test are green and unchanged (the base route runs below match B9a within noise).

- Base seed 1 was 16.3% for the expert in the B9a report; 18.0% now is inside the noise (one point is 3 runs; the standard error is about 2 points). Rusher and grinder are identical.
- The expert wins about a third less often with all Masterworks in the pool. The bot values the new parts by hand (3 to 4.5 each, above its take bar), so it drafts them; the ones it takes most are Night Watchman (93 of 900 runs) and Free Pawl (62), and runs holding those win least (6.5% and 4.9% against 10.8% overall). The bot does not know how to use them; that is a bot limit more than an item verdict. B10 may want a better drafter before reading the lift as an item effect.

## Plating-drafting route (plater)

A new route policy, `plater` (`src/sim/strat/v2routes.ts`, `decide.ts`): the expert's route with Plating parts valued 2 points higher in drafting and the turtle bot for combat. Main plan over 900 runs: **plating 834, burst 66** (base profile: plating 282 of 300). The other three policies stay at 98 to 99% burst (expert 897 of 900, rusher 890, grinder 896 burst; plating 3, 10 and 4). So the Clockmaker's memory is no longer a constant in sim: expert, rusher and grinder histories give the Governor Cap, plater histories give the Pierce Drill. The plater itself wins 0 to 0.3% (it reaches act 1's end: mean act 1.1): the turtle's slow damage cannot beat the Foreman, which is what the target-3 turtle result predicts. Pressure and statuses plans still never occur as a main plan.
