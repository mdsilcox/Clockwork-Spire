# v2 climb: route policies and the B8.5 tune (seed 1)

Date: 2026-10-05. Rules: the climb core (B8) with the v1 wardens still in place (B9 makes them frames). Journeyman, no meta progression.

## Result in one line

No content or constant change was needed. BV3, BV8 and BV11 are green with margin on seeds 1, 2 and 3 with the climb core and economy as merged (12 hours, rooms and Scrap prices as shipped). The failures that blocked them were bugs in the bots, not in the numbers.

## BV11: route policies (300 runs each)

| Seed | Expert | Rusher | Grinder | Expert minus rusher |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (the test's seed) | 14.7% | 7.7% | 1.3% | 7.0 points |
| 2 | 15.0% | 8.7% | 0.3% | 6.3 points |
| 3 | 14.7% | 10.0% | 0.0% | 4.7 points |

Mean act reached: expert 2.3 to 2.4, rusher 2.1 to 2.3, grinder 1.3 to 1.4. (These numbers moved by 2 to 4 points when the climb decisions were split out for the in-game autoplay: the fuse preview no longer consumes the run's reward stream. The ordering is unchanged.) All three use the v2 expert's combat search (a narrower beam, roughly 10 ms a turn, so 900 climbs finish in about 5 minutes; the fight report uses the full beam). Only the route differs.

- **Rusher**: shortest unlocked path to the door, rings the bell at once (Prepared 2, about 45 Scrap), never rests.
- **Grinder**: nearest uncleared fight room until none is left or midnight; the warden is then Overwound. Rests below 50% HP.
- **Expert**: values rooms by what they give per hour (fights for salvage, oil when below 85% HP, workbench and trader when it has Scrap to spend, events, vaults when it holds a key), keeps 4 hours in hand for the bell, avoids elites when below 65% HP, rings when 4, 5 or 6 fights (acts 1 to 3) are cleared and it is above 80% HP, or when nothing worth the detour is left.

What the sweep showed (seed 1, 300 runs, expert / rusher / grinder win rate):
- The bell is strong: a rusher that arrives with 7 hours left is Prepared +2 and wins 9%, nearly as often as an expert that builds for 12 hours and rings with about 4. The expert's edge comes from healing before the warden and from the salvage and trader parts it keeps.
- Hours move little. Act 2 and 3 at 14 or 16 hours: 14 to 16% for the expert against 14 to 15% at 12. Only act 1 matters (most runs end at the Foreman); 14 to 16 hours there lifts the expert to 19 to 20% but breaks AD4 (12 hours in act 1), so it was not kept.
- Room counts (two workbenches and two traders) moved the expert by one point; halving upgrade and remove prices moved it by one point. Both are inside the noise, so neither was kept.
- The expert leaves about 220 Scrap unspent at the end of a run, most of it paid by the warden and the bell, so prices do not bind.

|---|---|---|---|

Seeds 2 and 3 agree (from the previous bins: act 1 normals 15.9% to 16.5% for turtle and burst; elites at least 2.4x; BV8 at most 17%). The expert decides in at most 26 ms a turn (BV6).

### The regression, explained

After the climb-core merge BV3 normals failed in all three acts. The cause was in the sim, not the content: `collectBins` gathered its bins by playing careers with the v1 run driver (`playStratCareer`), and a real run config (`runConfigFor`, no `legacyMap`) now starts in the climb's `section` phase, which that driver does not handle. The runs stalled without fighting, so the bins held next to nothing and the replays measured empty fights. Fix: bins now come from expert climbs (`src/sim/strat/climb.ts`, the route expert), which play every phase through the real run API.

### Other bot defects found and fixed along the way

1. The expert, turtle and burst read a legacy warden's intents as empty (a legacy enemy's v2 intent carries no actions; only `e.intent` has the numbers). Against the Foreman and later wardens the expert was blind to incoming damage (Foreman win rate 15%; after the fix 69%).
2. The turtle credited Plating only against intents that survive the machine run. Killing an enemy removed its intents, which erased the credit, so at 2 core HP the turtle preferred to keep "absorbing" and stalled for 40 turns on one Pipe Snake bin. That single fight put the act 2 normal share at 75%. It now credits against the intents as shown before the run. BV8 went from failing in all three acts (B7.4 numbers: 40%, 44% and 77% on normals, 83% on an act 2 elite) to at most 13% on normals.
3. Part values for the v2 parts (Pry Bar, Cold Chisel, Auger, Wedge, Mending Spool, Soothing Valve and the locked Core Drill, Sapper, Sunder) were missing, so the expert drafted them at value 1 and left them in the salvage tray.

## Changes to content and constants

None. The tune levers (hours per act, Scrap prices, room counts, act 1 regular enemy numbers) were tried as above and none was kept. The expert's win rate is about 15% with the v1 wardens, over the 5% line of rules 7.4 target 1. That target is for B10 after B9 turns the wardens into frames; the cause here is the legacy wardens being easy for the machine the expert builds, not the climb numbers.

## Autoplay (in-game career test)

`window.__game.autoplay` now plays the climb: `src/sim/strat/decide.ts` returns the next action as data, the simulator carries it out on the core (`climb.ts`) and `src/app/autoplay.ts` carries it out through the controller's player actions (move, ring, key, lock, workbench, trader, oil, salvage, target taps, place, swap, run). Combat is the v2 expert with the route beam. A fresh-save career wins on run 3 (`npm run test:career`: about 1 s of play, 8 s for the test).
